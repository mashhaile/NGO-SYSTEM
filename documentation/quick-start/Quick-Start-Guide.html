NGO Management System

Quick Start Guide: step by step
Web address: https://ngo-system-e5af.onrender.com
Your login: sent to you separately (email and password)
Works in: Chrome, Edge or Firefox, on a computer or tablet
Version 1.0 · October 2026

This guide shows the main steps for trying the system. Page 2 explains how to sign in and what each type of user can do. Page 3 shows the order of work. The rest of the guide walks through each step.

1. Sign in

  1. Open https://ngo-system-e5af.onrender.com in your browser.
  2. Enter the Email and Password you were given, then click Sign in.
  3. The Dashboard opens. The menu on the left shows only what your login is allowed to use.
  4. To leave, open the menu under your name at the top right and choose Logout.
First visit of the day: the site may take up to one minute to open while the server starts. After that it is fast.
Forgot your password? Ask your organization administrator to reset it. You will be asked to choose a new password at your next sign-in.

What each user type can do

User typeCan do
Organization AdminEverything: settings, users and roles, lists (lookups), programs, projects, approvals, reports.
Finance OfficerBudgets, expenses, donors and donations, including approving budgets and expenses.
Data EntryDaily records: beneficiaries, enrollments, enterprises, terms of reference, activity runs, attendance, service deliveries, expenses (to be approved by finance).
ViewerCan look at everything and run reports, but cannot change anything.

The administrator can also create custom roles (Administration › Roles) and choose exactly what each one may do.

2. How the work flows

Work in this order. Each step uses what the earlier steps created, so the lists you need appear in the forms.

1 · Set upOrganization, users, lists (lookups)
2 · PlanPrograms, projects, budgets, terms of reference, activities
3 · FundDonors and donations
4 · Do the workBeneficiaries, activity runs, attendance, service delivery, expenses
5 · ReportDashboard and reports, with Excel/PDF export

Statuses: what they mean

StatusMeaning
PlannedNot started yet.
ActiveRunning: everything can be added.
SuspendedPaused: nothing new can be added until it is Active again.
CompletedFinished: no new plans, but late records (deliveries, attendance, expenses) can still be added.
CancelledStopped: nothing new can be added, and it is not counted in reports.

Budgets, terms of reference and expenses go through approval: Draft → Submitted → Approved (or Returned for changes).

3. Set up (Organization Admin, once)

  1. Administration › Organization: check your organization's name, contact and logo.
  2. Administration › User Management › Create User: create a login for each staff member and choose their role.
  3. Administration › Lookup Management: review the lists used in forms (service types, items, units, activity types, gender and others), and add your own.
  4. Administration › Custom Fields (optional): add extra questions to the beneficiary form, for example "Disability type".
  5. Administration › Wording (optional): use your organization's own words, for example "Fund" instead of "Donation", or "Ongoing" instead of "Active". Screens and reports change at once.

4. Plan the work

  1. Programs › Programs › Add Program: name, dates, target and status.
  2. Programs › Projects › Add Project: choose the program, set the donor, manager and dates. For a project in phases, open the project and use Add Phase in the Phases card.
  3. Finance › Budgets › New Budget: budget lines per year (fiscal year = January–December). Click Submit for Approval. A finance officer or admin then Approves it or Returns it for changes.
  4. Activities › Terms of Reference › New TOR: write the TOR for an activity and submit it. Once approved, the activity can be created from it.
  5. Activities › Activities › Create Activity: the activity under its project (dates must be inside the project's dates).
  6. Activities › Activity Runs › Create Activity Run: each batch or round of the activity, with location and dates.
Budgets: each line shows its period, total and approval status.

5. Record funding

  1. Funding › Donors › Add Donor: the donor's name, type and contact.
  2. Funding › Donations › Add Donation: donor, amount, currency (ETB, USD, EUR, GBP or CHF) and the program or project it funds. For a foreign currency, enter the exchange rate on the day it was received. Reports also show the value in ETB.
  3. Status: Pledged (promised) or Received. Only received money counts as funding received.

6. Register beneficiaries

  1. Beneficiaries › Beneficiaries › Add Beneficiary: name, gender, date of birth, phone, and location (choose Region, then Zone, then Woreda).
  2. For many people at once, click Import Excel, then Download Template. Fill in the template and upload it. Rows with problems are listed so you can correct them.
  3. Beneficiaries › Enrollments › Create Enrollment: enroll each person in the program they belong to.
  4. For groups and businesses: Enterprises (the group) and Enterprise Members (its members).
Beneficiaries: search, filter, open, edit, or import from Excel.

7. Record what was done

Trainings and meetings: attendance

  1. Activities › Attendance › Create Attendance: choose the activity run and the session date.
  2. Open the session's sheet and mark each person Present, Late, Excused or Absent. Enrolled people are listed for you.
  3. You can also add other participants: staff, partners, government officials and community members.

Goods, cash and services: service delivery

  1. Services › Service Delivery › Create Service Delivery: choose who receives it (individual, household, group or institution) and how it is shared.
  2. Choose the program (and project, activity or run if it belongs to one), the date and the location.
  3. Add the items (for example hygiene kits or cash) with quantity, unit cost and currency, and pick the people or groups who received them.
  4. If the items were bought with an expense, link that expense so the cost is not counted twice.

8. Record spending

  1. Finance › Expenses › Record Expense: the budget line, amount, date, payment method, receipt or invoice number, and the donation that pays for it.
  2. Submit it. A finance officer or admin Approves it (it then counts as spent and is locked) or Returns it for changes. A wrong approved expense can be Voided.
  3. If a line would go over budget, the system warns you. You can still submit it, but the approver must confirm and give a reason.

9. See results: dashboard and reports

  1. Dashboard: the key numbers at a glance.
  2. Reports: choose a report (overview, beneficiaries, programs and projects, activities and training, service delivery, enterprises, donors and funding, budget and spending).
  3. Choose the period (year, half year, quarter, month, date range or all time) and filters such as program, project, region and gender.
  4. Click any row to open that record's full report. Download with Excel, PDF or CSV.

10. Good to know

The system checks your entries

  • Dates: an end date cannot be before its start date. Records must fall inside their parent's dates (for example an activity inside its project's dates). Completed work cannot have a future date.
  • Status: nothing new can be added to suspended or cancelled work, and inactive people or donors cannot receive anything new.
  • Money: each amount keeps its own currency. Totals are shown per currency, plus the ETB value where a rate is known.
  • If something is refused, the message says which field to check and why.

Tips

  • Each list has Search, filters and Reset. Click a name to open the record.
  • Codes (BEN-000123, PRG-000001-PRJ-002 …) are created automatically. Use them when talking about a record.
  • Everything you change is saved immediately and recorded in the history (audit log).
Trying the system: the demo organization already has sample data (500 beneficiaries, programs, projects, donations, budgets and reports). Feel free to add, change and approve records. Everything you enter is real in the demo and can be reset afterwards.
